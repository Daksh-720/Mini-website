Mobile Buying Guide

  • Processor: Prefer Snapdragon or MediaTek Dimensity for best performance.
  • RAM: Minimum 6GB for smooth usage.
  • Battery: 4500mAh or more recommended.
  • Camera: Sensor quality matters more than megapixels.
  • Display: AMOLED preferred; 90Hz/120Hz for smoother experience.

Laptop Buying Guide

  • Processor: Intel i5/Ryzen 5 or better.
  • RAM: At least 8GB; 16GB for programming or editing.
  • Storage: SSD (256GB+) is a must.
  • Graphics: Integrated for normal use; dedicated for gaming/editing.
  • Display: Full HD (1080p) with anti-glare preferred.

General Buying Tips

  • Always compare prices on multiple online stores.
  • Check reviews and user ratings before buying.
  • Look for warranty and return policy.
  • Buy only from trusted and verified sellers.
